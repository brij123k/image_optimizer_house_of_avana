What this app does

image-optimizer compresses and renames the product images in your Shopify store, writes ALT text for them with AI, and suggests keywords. To do that it reads your products and images and, when you ask it to, replaces or renames images.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Your store's domain, name, country, currency and Shopify plan, and the owner's name and email, as Shopify reports them at installTo identify your account and show it back to you
An access token from ShopifyTo read and update your product images on your behalf. It is stored on our server with restricted access
Your product titles and product imagesRead to compress, rename and label them. The app keeps counts and keywords, not copies of your images
Usage counters, suggested keywords and a record of which images were processedTo show your dashboard and avoid processing an image twice

We do not collect or store information about your store's customers or orders.

Who else receives data

  • Shopify – the platform the app runs on and the source of your store data.
  • Google (Gemini API) – when you use AI labeling, the product image and its title are sent to Google to generate ALT text, file names and keywords.
  • ipwho.is – visitor IP addresses are sent to this service to find an approximate city for your access history.
  • Our hosting provider – the servers the app runs on.

We do not sell your data.

Keeping and deleting data

  • When you uninstall the app, your access token is deleted straight away.
  • Shopify then tells us to delete your store's remaining data (48 hours after uninstall), and we do: your store record, usage counters, keywords, access history and processing records.
  • We keep purchase records (store domain, plan, price, date) because we must retain financial records.
  • Because we hold no data about your customers, requests to access or delete a shopper's data need no action from us.

Your choices

You can uninstall the app at any time. To ask what we hold about your store, or to have it deleted sooner, email {{ email }}.

Good to know

  • Originals are replaced. Compressing an image replaces it in your store, and the original cannot be restored. Try one product first.
  • Renaming changes the image URL. A renamed image gets a new address, and the old one stops working. Anything linking to the old address, such as blog posts or emails, will break.
  • Credits. Each image compressed, renamed or labeled uses one credit. Every store starts with 100 free, and credit packs never expire.
  • Where to find it. Open the app from your Shopify admin under Apps.

Uninstalling

Remove the app from your Shopify admin (Settings → Apps). Your stored data is deleted as described in the privacy policy.
